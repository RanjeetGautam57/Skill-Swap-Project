About Skill Swap
Share Your Knowledge, Discover New Skills

We believe learning becomes better when people learn from each other. Skill Swap helps you find people with different skills, exchange knowledge, and build meaningful connections while learning something new.
Why we built this
Access to good-quality learning
is often gated by cost.

Professional courses, private tutors, and workshops charge fees that not everyone can afford, even when the person on the other side would happily teach in exchange for learning something themselves.
Our principles

Mutual, not one-sided.
Every swap benefits both people — there's no "customer" and "provider."

Trust is earned, not assumed.
Ratings are tied to a specific completed session, not a general profile score.

A shared skill vocabulary.
Skills come from a curated list, so "React" and "ReactJS" don't fragment into two entities.

OUR MISSION
Making Learning More Accessible
Our mission is to make learning more accessible, collaborative, and community-driven. Skill Swap aims to create a space where people can freely exchange knowledge, discover new opportunities, and support each other's growth without making learning feel complicated or expensive.
