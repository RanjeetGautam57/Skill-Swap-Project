How the board works
Everything you need to know before you pin your first skill.

THE IDEA

Knowledge, traded directly

Most learning platforms want your money. Skill Swap wants your time and
something you already know — that's the whole trade.

1

Build your two-sided profile

Every profile has two lists: Skills I Can Teach and Skills I Want to Learn, each picked from our curated skill categories with a proficiency or desired level attached — so a match is never a guessing game.

2

Browse or get matched

Search the board by skill category, or let the system surface people whose "teaches" list complements your "wants" list — and whose "wants" list you can fill in return.

3

Send a swap request

Propose exactly what you're offering and what you want in return, with a short note. The other person accepts, rejects, or counters.

4

Schedule & meet

Once accepted, agree on a date, time, and mode — online or in person — and use the built-in chat to sort out the details.

5

Complete the session

Mark the session complete when you're done. This unlocks reviews for both sides — reviews are always tied to a real, finished swap.

6

Rate each other

Leave an honest rating and note. This is what keeps the board trustworthy without any money on the table to enforce it.

GRAND RULES

What keeps the board fair

One skill list, curated

Skills come from an admin-maintained master list, not free text — so "React" and "ReactJS" don't split into two dead-end entries.

Reviews are session-bound

You can only review a partner after a session you actually completed together — no drive-by ratings.

Every account is approved

New sign-ups are reviewed before they can send or receive swap requests, keeping the board spam-free.
