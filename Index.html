The Process

Four Steps, One Skill Swap

A simple and friendly way to share what you know, learn something new, and connect with others.
1.
List Your Skills

Share what you know and what you want to learn.
Choose the skills you can teach and the skills you want to learn from our curated list.

2.
Find Your Match

Connect with the right learning partner
Browse users whose skills match your learn and whose interests complement what you can teach..

3.
Request & Schedule.

Send a request and plan your session.
Choose a suitable partner, send a swap request, agree on a time, and meet online or in person..

4.
Learn & Rate

Complete your session and share your experience.
Learn together, exchange knowledge, and rating to help build a trusted Skill Swap community.
