THE IDEA

Knowledge, traded directly

Most learning platforms want your money. Skill Swap wants your time and
something you already know — that's the whole trade.

GRAND RULES

What keeps the board fair

One skill list, curated

Skills come from an admin-maintained master list, not free text — so "React" and "ReactJS" don't split into two dead-end entries.

Reviews are session-bound

You can only review a partner after a session you actually completed together — no drive-by ratings.

Every account is approved

New sign-ups are reviewed before they can send or receive swap requests, keeping the board spam-free.
